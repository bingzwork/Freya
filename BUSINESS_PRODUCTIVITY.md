## Overview

Business & Productivity capabilities extend Freya beyond software engineering into practical day-to-day work.

The current implementation focuses almost entirely on software engineering.

Business automation, client management, documentation, project management, and productivity workflows have not yet been implemented.

These capabilities are intended to transform Freya into a complete AI work assistant capable of assisting developers, freelancers, startups, and businesses.
