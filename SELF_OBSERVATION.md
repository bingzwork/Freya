## Overview

Freya contains a mature self-observation foundation.

Multiple monitoring systems already exist for health reporting, diagnostics, auditing, confidence evaluation, project metrics, and runtime monitoring.

Most systems operate independently.

The next stage of development is integrating them into a unified autonomous decision pipeline.
