## Overview

Creative Capabilities represent Freya's future expansion beyond software engineering and productivity.

This pillar is intended to enable Freya to assist with writing, design, media production, content creation, brainstorming, and other creative tasks while maintaining the same autonomous workflow principles used for engineering.
